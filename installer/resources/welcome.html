Surround Bridge

Surround Bridge sends everything your Mac plays to your amp, receiver or soundbar as Dolby Digital or DTS surround, over HDMI or optical, and turns stereo into 5.1 on the way.

This installs the Surround Bridge app in your Applications folder. It lives in the menu bar (🔊); there you switch it on and set the surround levels.

The first time you switch it on, Surround Bridge helps you install BlackHole 2ch, a free virtual audio device by Existential Audio that your apps play into. It's downloaded from its official website and installed with its own installer.

If macOS says this installer is from an unidentified developer: right-click it in Finder, choose Open, then Open again.
