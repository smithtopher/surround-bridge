Surround Bridge is installed

It has opened in the menu bar: look for the speaker icon (🔊) at the top right of the screen.

  1. Click it and switch Surround Bridge on.
  2. The first time, allow it access to the "Surround Bridge" audio device (macOS asks as if it were a microphone).
  3. Pick your TV, receiver or soundbar in Settings if it isn't chosen already.

Everything your Mac plays now reaches your amp as surround. Switch it off and your sound goes back to where it was.
